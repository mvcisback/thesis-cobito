Workspaces and Decision Processes

You have brains in your head. You have feet in your shoes. You can steer yourself in any direction you choose.

The previous chapter reviewed machinery for representing concepts and formal languages. This chapter reviews machinery for predicting the behavior of agents acting in stochastic environments, starting with a few useful probability distributions. We assume familiarity with elementary probability theory.

Markov decision processes

We model an agent acting in a stochastic domain as a Markov decision process.

Markov decision process
